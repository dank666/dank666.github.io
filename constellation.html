Constellation

One star for every day the plan got done. No day is ever taken away for being missed — a gap is just a gap, never a broken star — so the sky only ever grows. It's a slow, public way of keeping myself honest.

每完成一天计划中的事，就点亮一颗星星。漏掉的一天不会摘掉任何星星——只是那天没有新的星光，从不惩罚——所以这片天空只会越来越绚烂。这是我用来监督自己的一种慢办法。

  • –stars lit颗星星
  • –day streak天连续
  • –best streak最长纪录

No stars yet — the sky is waiting. 还没有星星——天空在等待第一颗。

The sky couldn't be loaded right now. 星图暂时无法加载。

Hover a star for its date and note. Bigger, warmer stars mark milestones (7, 30, 100, 365 days…). 把鼠标移到星星上可以看到日期和备注。更大更暖的星星是里程碑（第 7、30、100、365 天……）。
